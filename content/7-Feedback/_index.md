### Overall Evaluation
**1. Working Environment**  
- The environment is open and practical. Interns work directly on real AWS accounts and are responsible for the cost and security of the systems they build. The events and workshops at the AWS office let me meet industry professionals and other interns.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
- The mentors and team admins gave timely support when our team needed to confirm requirements and get the project architecture reviewed. The program's lab path and materials are enough for self-study. The team still mainly researched things on its own, which helped me build my ability to solve problems independently.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
- Very relevant to my major in Artificial Intelligence. I was able to apply my knowledge of time series data, model evaluation and large language models, while also learning what university rarely covers: deploying models to run for real on the cloud, operating them and controlling cost.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
- There was a lot to learn: from core AWS services (Lambda, S3, DynamoDB, API Gateway, Cognito, Bedrock), infrastructure as code and CI, to teamwork skills through GitHub (Pull Requests and cross-review). Events such as AWS Cloud Day helped me keep up with new trends like AI agents.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
- Our five-person team had clearly divided roles, was ready to support each other and gave frank feedback through reviews. Shared activities such as the volunteering event brought everyone closer outside of work.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
- Interns receive AWS credit to build a real project, can join AWS events and workshops for free, and have a clear lab path. Working hours are fairly flexible and fit around class schedules.


### Additional Questions
**What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
- Building a real, working product end to end on AWS that users can access, instead of only doing exercises on my own machine. I am also satisfied that I learned how to work with a process in a team.

**What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**  
- Announce the grading format, criteria and deadline for the final deliverable earlier and in more detail, so teams can plan from the start. A few mid-term checkpoints with mentors to give feedback on architecture and cost would help teams avoid many time-consuming directions.

**If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**  
- Yes. The program suits anyone who wants to learn cloud and AI in a practical way: you use a real AWS account, work on a team project with a concrete product, and join many professional events. The difficulty, however, is that you need to be proactive about self-study, because most of the knowledge has to be researched on your own.


### Suggestions & Expectations
**Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
- The program could share some sample projects from previous cohorts and a security and cost checklist for team projects from the first week. In addition, if a mid-term architecture review were held, teams would get more feedback so that newcomers are not caught off guard when working on the project.

**Would you like to continue this program in the future?**  
- Yes. I want to learn more deeply about deploying and operating AI on AWS, especially AI agents and MLOps, and I hope to have the chance to take part in AWS's future activities or programs.

**Any other comments (free sharing):**  
- I have no other comments. Thank you to FCAJ, the admin team and the mentors (especially Mr. Hào) for always taking care of me and my team throughout our learning and real project work.
