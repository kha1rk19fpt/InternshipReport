During my internship at **AWS First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** from **14/09/2026** to **14/12/2026**, I had the opportunity to learn, practice, and apply the knowledge from university in a real working environment.  
I took part in the **CPAWS** project - a platform forecasting gold, silver, Brent and WTI prices with an explanatory chatbot on Amazon Bedrock - as the **Research Lead** responsible for the forecasting model. Through the project, I improved my skills in using AWS Cloud infrastructure and applying it to real-world problems.

I always tried to complete my tasks well, follow the rules, and actively communicate with teammates and mentors to work more effectively.

### Strengths
- **Self-learning and quick to pick things up:** When the internship started, I had almost no hands-on experience with AWS services specifically, or with Cloud Computing in general. I read the documentation myself, did the labs in the FCAJ learning path and applied them right away to my work, which helped me grasp the core services in a short time.
- **Careful and responsible for the work I take on:** I usually double-check results before reporting, record the reasoning behind each decision, and am not afraid to say clearly when results are not good yet.
- **Proactive communication:** When I run into a problem, I research it first before asking, and I try to offer a concrete option instead of only pointing out the difficulty. I am also ready to help my teammates when needed.
- **Team management:** I usually split tasks and set deadlines for myself and the whole team, which helps push progress forward and lets me make sure everyone finishes their work within the time I assign.

### Areas for Improvement
- **Balancing thoroughness with doing just enough.** Sometimes I spent too much time on a single detail, when a simpler solution would already have met the requirement.
- **Keeping notes regularly.** In some weeks I wrote down my work late, which made it easy to miss or misremember details and could lead to mistakes in my work and in writing the worklogs.

### Reflection
- The internship at FCAJ changed how I see work. I used to think that building a product that runs was enough; now I understand that a good product also needs to be easy to operate, secure, measurable, and correctly understood by others.
- I also learned how to work in a team with a process: clear roles, cross-review and recorded decisions. At first these things felt slow, but I gradually realized they help the whole team avoid many mistakes and understand each other better.
- What I value most is honesty in work: stating exactly what I have and have not managed to do. This is a habit I want to keep when I start working. I thank my mentors, the program organizers and my teammates for being with me over the past three months.
