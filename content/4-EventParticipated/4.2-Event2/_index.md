# Event: "AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi – Watch Party: Ho Chi Minh City"

### Event Overview

- **Event Name:** AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi – Watch Party: Ho Chi Minh City  
- **Date:** 29/09/2026  
- **Role:** Attendee  
- **Format:** Watch party: following the Hanoi event live from the Ho Chi Minh City venue  
- **Location:** 26th floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City  
- **Agenda:** [Event agenda](https://aws.amazon.com/events/cloud-day-hanoi/agenda/)


### Program Highlights

#### Opening Keynote
The keynote centered on one question: what does it take to solve problems everyone thinks are impossible? My key notes:
- Innovation starts from customer problems, not from technology
- Generative AI and agentic AI are moving from experimentation to creating real value in businesses
- Global infrastructure and managed services let even small teams build big products
- Vietnamese companies are accelerating their digital transformation on AWS

#### Agentic AI: From Demo to Deployment
- **The gap between demo and production:** an agent that works well in a demo may not be reliable on real data; it needs testing, monitoring and limits on its permissions
- **Agents for software development:** AI can write code, write tests, review and document, but humans remain ultimately responsible for quality
- **Large-scale migration with AI:** how AWS Transform uses agents to help modernize and migrate legacy applications

#### AI-Ready Data Foundations
- Scattered, non-standardized data is the biggest barrier when companies bring AI into their products
- A data lake on Amazon S3 as the foundation, combined with data governance (catalog, access control, data quality)
- An AI model is only as good as its input data: you need to trace where data comes from and how it was processed

#### Application Modernization and Platform Engineering
- A Vietnamese bank's story of building an Internal Developer Platform on Amazon EKS
- Choosing the right tool for the right problem: a complex architecture is not always needed; sometimes a simple solution is more effective and cheaper
- An introduction to Amazon Quick with an AI-assistant building challenge for attendees

### Key Technical Takeaways

- **Agents need "guardrails":** least-privilege IAM permissions, a limited tool scope, and logging every action for later review
- **Evaluate before trusting:** a fixed test question set is needed to measure agent quality, not just a few manual tries
- **Multi-layer data lake:** separate raw, cleaned and feature data, with clear rules for each layer
- **Serverless and managed services:** help small teams focus on the product instead of operating servers

### Personal Learnings

#### Technical Growth
- I understood better that the hard part of real-world AI lies in data, testing and operations, not only in the model
- The part on evaluating agents with a fixed question set helped me picture how our team could test the project's chatbot

#### Industry Insights
- Vietnamese companies, especially in banking and finance, are investing heavily in data platforms and AI
- Demand is growing for engineers who understand data, models and cloud together

### Event Experience

This was the first big AWS event I attended since starting my internship. The atmosphere was lively, and hearing real stories first-hand showed me how valuable what I was learning in the Labs becomes once applied in companies. I mostly listened, took notes and followed the presentations; many of those notes later went into my discussions with the team when designing the CPAWS architecture.

The event also made me more confident in our team's direction: a useful AI product does not have to use the most complex model, but it needs trustworthy data, honest evaluation and a stable system.
