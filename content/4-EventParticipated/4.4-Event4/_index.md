# Event: "Smart Plushies for Children"

### Event Overview

- **Event Name:** Smart Plushies for Children (AWS Volunteering event)  
- **Date:** October 26, 2026  
- **Role:** Volunteer  
- **Format:** Volunteering activity  
- **Location:** 36th floor, Ho Chi Minh City  

This was a volunteering activity organized by AWS HCM, inviting employees and interns to make plush toys together as gifts for children. Each toy was packed with a STEM kit so the children could try building a STEM project themselves.

### Activities

#### Opening
- The organizers introduced the purpose of the program: bringing joy and inspiration for learning STEM to children
- Instructions on how to make the plush toys

#### Making the Plush Toys
- We split into small groups (2 people), each receiving a set of materials: toy shells, stuffing and a STEM kit
- Assembling and testing the STEM kit in advance
- Finishing the details, taking the STEM kit apart and checking quality before packing

#### Packing with the STEM Kit
- Each plush toy was packed with a STEM kit that the group had assembled in advance
- Writing encouraging notes and instructions for the children
- Packing and gathering the finished gifts for the organizers to deliver

### What I Learned

#### Community Spirit
- Technology is not only about products and services; tech companies can also help pass on a love of science to the next generation
- A small gift with a STEM learning card can be the start of a child's curiosity

### Event Experience

This was my first time making a plush toy by hand and assembling a STEM kit. I was quite clumsy at first, but with guidance from the staff, my partner and I gradually got used to it and finished our toys. Seeing the boxes of gifts packed, knowing they would reach the children, was a memorable feeling.
