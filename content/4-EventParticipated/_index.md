During my internship, I took part in three events with AWS and the AWS FCAJ HCM community: two professional events and one volunteering activity. At the professional events I attended as a listener, following the sessions and taking notes; at the volunteering activity I worked hands-on making plush toys with everyone.

---

### [Event 1 – AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026](4.1-Event1/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026 (Ho Chi Minh City venue)  
&emsp;**Date:** 29/09/2026  
&emsp;**Location:** Floor 26, Bitexco Financial Tower, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  
&emsp;**Format:** Full-day conference, keynote and breakout sessions  

&emsp;**Content:** AWS's flagship annual event in Vietnam, focused on agentic AI, AI-ready data foundations, application modernization and cloud migration, with real stories from Vietnamese companies.  
&emsp;**Outcome:** I better understood why clean, governed data is a prerequisite for any AI application, and applied that mindset to how the CPAWS team built its price data pipeline.

---

### [Event 2 – Meet the AWS CTO](4.2-Event2/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** Meet the AWS CTO with the AWS FCAJ HCM community  
&emsp;**Date:** 02/10/2026  
&emsp;**Location:** Floor 26, Bitexco Financial Tower, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  
&emsp;**Format:** In-person talk and conversation  

&emsp;**Content:** A talk on Amazon's builder culture, designing simple, resilient and cost-aware systems, the role of AI in software engineering, and advice for students starting their careers.  
&emsp;**Outcome:** I took away the lesson that "cost is a design requirement", which led our team to choose batch prediction with no 24/7 resources.

---

### [Event 3 – STEM Plush Toys with AWS HCM](4.3-Event3/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** STEM plush toys with AWS HCM  
&emsp;**Date:** 26/10/2026  
&emsp;**Location:** Floor 36, Ho Chi Minh City  
&emsp;**Role:** Volunteer  
&emsp;**Format:** Volunteering activity  

&emsp;**Content:** Volunteers stuffed, sewed and finished plush toys, then packed them with STEM learning cards as gifts for children, encouraging their curiosity about science and technology.  
&emsp;**Outcome:** A meaningful experience beyond technical work that showed me how technology and community connect, and strengthened my teamwork with people I had just met.
