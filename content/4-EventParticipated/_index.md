During my internship, I took part in five events with AWS and the AWS FCAJ HCM community: four professional events and one volunteering activity.

---

### [Event 1 – Buildrathon Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global](4.1-event1/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** Buildrathon Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global  
&emsp;**Date:** 26/09/2026  
&emsp;**Location:** Bitexco Financial Tower, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  
&emsp;**Format:** Half-day kickoff: introduction, talkshow, Q&A and a short hands-on challenge  

&emsp;**Content:** Kickoff of the Buildrathon organized with CMC Global: the challenge and judging criteria, an overview of AWS Cloud, generative AI and AI agents, a career talkshow, and a short hands-on AI Agent Challenge.  
&emsp;**Outcome:** A first map of the building blocks of a generative AI application on AWS (prompts, RAG, knowledge bases, agents).

---

### [Event 2 – AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi – Watch Party](4.2-event2/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi – Watch Party: Ho Chi Minh City  
&emsp;**Date:** 29/09/2026  
&emsp;**Location:** 26th floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  
&emsp;**Format:** Watch party: following the Hanoi event live from the Ho Chi Minh City venue  

&emsp;**Content:** AWS's largest annual event in Vietnam, focused on agentic AI, AI-ready data foundations, application modernization and cloud migration, with real stories from Vietnamese companies.  
&emsp;**Outcome:** I better understood why clean, governed data is a prerequisite for any AI application.

### [Event 3 – Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner](4.3-event3/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner (AWS CTO), with the AWS FCAJ HCM community  
&emsp;**Date:** 02/10/2026  
&emsp;**Location:** 26th floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  
&emsp;**Format:** In-person talk and conversation  

&emsp;**Content:** A talk on Amazon's "builder" culture, designing simple, resilient and cost-efficient systems, the role of AI in a software engineer's work, and advice for students starting their careers.

### [Event 5 – Cloud Mastery](4.5-event5/)

&emsp;**Event Name:** Cloud Mastery  
&emsp;**Date:** 14/11/2026  
&emsp;**Location:** 26th floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  
&emsp;**Format:** Technical talk  

&emsp;**Content:** Taking AI from prototype to production: four pillars on AWS (security, scalability, reliability, cost) and the components of Amazon Bedrock AgentCore (Runtime, Identity, Gateway, Memory, Observability).  
&emsp;**Outcome:** I learned that a working model is only the starting point, and how to separate inbound and outbound permissions for an agent and log and measure every run.
